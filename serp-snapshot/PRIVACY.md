# Privacy Policy — SERP Snapshot

_Last updated: 2026-07-26_

**SERP Snapshot does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. Everything
the extension does happens locally in your browser.**

## What the extension accesses

- **The content of the Google search results page you are viewing**, and only
  while you are on `https://www.google.com/search…` or
  `https://www.google.co.in/search…`. It reads the page to extract organic
  results, People Also Ask, related searches, and AI Overview citations when you
  click **Capture**.

## What the extension stores

- **Your settings** (default export format and section toggles) and a **local
  history of your last 20 captures** (query text, capture time, result count,
  and whether an AI Overview was present) are saved with Chrome's `storage` API
  **on your own device**. This never leaves your machine and is not synced to us.

## What the extension does NOT do

- No network requests of any kind. No servers, APIs, telemetry, or analytics.
- No cookies, no advertising identifiers, no fingerprinting.
- No collection of personal or financial information.
- No selling or sharing of any data with third parties.

## Permissions

- **`storage`** — used only to persist your settings and local capture history.
- **Host access to Google search pages** (`www.google.com/search*`,
  `www.google.co.in/search*`) — used only to read the current results page so it
  can be exported. The extension runs on no other sites.

## Your control

Exported CSV files and clipboard content are created on your device by your
action and go only where you send them. You can clear the local history at any
time from the extension's **History → Clear all** button, or by removing the
extension.
